## 3. Installations and slots

**On Gumroad — two installations per license.** Your license key gives you two slots: two computers running the app at the same time.

**Changing computers?** Open the tray menu on the old machine and choose **Deactivate this installation** — that frees the slot. Then install on the new one and activate it. Two minutes.

**Heads up.** If you don't deactivate before the change (you reformat, you replace the disk, you swap the laptop without thinking), the slot stays occupied on a machine you no longer use, and the new install consumes the second slot. **We do not restore slots.** Not by support request, not for hardware failure, not for any reason. The two slots are the entire entitlement of your license.

So: deactivate before you change anything. It's the only mechanism — and it's in your hands.

**On Microsoft Store — different rules.** Microsoft Store handles installations through your Microsoft account, with its own policy — effectively unlimited installations across your devices, within Microsoft's terms. The two-slot limit above does not apply to Microsoft Store purchases.

## 7. Offline behaviour

The app is built to be offline-friendly. After activation it carries an 8-day astronomical window for your city and runs without needing the internet most of the time.

You may see these states in the city name on the clock:

- **Normal.** Just the city. You're online or the cached data is current. Nothing to do.
- **`Offline / <City>`.** You went offline but the cached window still covers today. The clock is fully functional with the data it already has.
- **`First launch offline`.** It's the very first time the app launches and there's no internet. The app cannot activate without a connection for that first time. Reconnect and try again.

If the offline period stretches past the 8-day window, the clock falls into a **degraded display** — the city is hidden and the data shown is symbolic until the connection returns. The hands keep moving; you don't lose the time.

## 8. If your license key is misused

We protect the honest customer whose key got out — not the one using a key that isn't theirs.

If we detect a single license key being used on more devices than the slots allow, in a way that looks like cloning or sharing:

1. We notify you.
2. We revoke the affected installations.
3. **We issue you a new license key — once.** Your right to use the app is preserved; you reinstall with the new key on your devices.

If the same thing happens a second time on the same account, the license ends, and we do not issue a further key. The remediation is a one-time courtesy to a customer acting in good faith, not a recurring fix.

## 9. If we ever stop

RelojSolarLunar uses a small online check to validate your license. As long as we're operating, that check runs and you don't notice it.

If at some point we permanently shut the service down — we stop the business, we move on, life happens — **your perpetual license stays usable**. Before we close, we will publish a final update of the app that **removes the license check entirely**, so your installation keeps running on its own, indefinitely. You bought a perpetual license; we mean it.

## 7. Comportamiento offline

La app está pensada para funcionar bien sin conexión. Tras la activación lleva una ventana astronómica de 8 días para tu ciudad y se mueve sin necesidad de internet la mayor parte del tiempo.

Estos son los estados que puedes ver en el nombre de la ciudad en el reloj:

- **Normal.** Solo la ciudad. Estás conectado o los datos en caché siguen vigentes. Nada que hacer.
- **`Offline / <Ciudad>`.** Has perdido la conexión pero la ventana en caché todavía cubre hoy. El reloj funciona perfectamente con los datos que ya tiene.
- **`First launch offline`.** Es la primera vez que arranca la app y no hay internet. La app no puede activarse sin conexión esa primera vez. Vuelve a conectarte e inténtalo de nuevo.

Si el periodo sin conexión se alarga más allá de los 8 días, el reloj entra en **modo degradado** — la ciudad se oculta y los datos que muestra son simbólicos hasta que vuelva la conexión. Las manecillas siguen moviéndose; no pierdes la hora.
