Section 1

At a glance

Most things take care of themselves.

Buy. Activate. Run. The clock keeps time, validates itself in the background, and asks for nothing more. This page is for the rest — the few moments when you may want to know how something works, or reach a human.

Activation email not in your inbox? Check spam and promotions, and verify the email you used at purchase. Activation emails are issued by your purchase platform — Gumroad or Microsoft Store — at the moment of payment.
Section 2

Two themes, one app

One app. Two faces. Pergamino is warm and classic — parchment tones, serif typography, the feel of an old manuscript. Mundial 2026 is bold and alive — green field, gold accents, the energy of the cup. Same precision. Same data. Different soul.

Each theme is sold on its own. Want both? Buy both — they sit happily together on the same machine.

Two installations per license. Changing computers? Deactivate from the tray menu of the old one, then activate on the new. No fuss.

Section 3

One purchase, no updates

The version you buy is the version you keep.

RelojSolarLunar is sold once and owned forever. Not a subscription. Not a service. A finished product, complete on the day you bought it.

Because of that, there are no automatic updates. The copy on your machine today is the copy on your machine tomorrow — running quietly, doing what it has always done.

If we release a new edition someday — a refreshed theme, a different motif, an entirely new product — it will be a new purchase. Customers from earlier editions are not migrated, not nudged, not interrupted. What you bought is what you have.

Section 4

Billing & refunds

Your purchase platform handles the money. Gumroad or Microsoft Store — whichever you used — is the merchant on record. They charge you. They invoice you. They refund you. We just make the app.

Refunds

14 days from purchase. Request through the platform you bought from, following their standard refund flow.

Invoices

Available in your Gumroad library or your Microsoft account. We do not issue separate invoices.

Section 5

System requirements

Modest needs. Quiet behaviour.

The app is light by design — about 50 MB on disk, lives in the system tray, only reaches out to the internet when it has to.

ItemRequirement
WindowsWindows 10 (1809) or later, 64-bit
macOSmacOS 11 Big Sur or later — Gumroad only
Disk space~50 MB
InternetFor activation and periodic license check
Offline modeUp to 8 days of normal operation; degraded display thereafter
VPNs: the app reads your location from your IP. While the VPN is on, the location shown will be the VPN's exit, not yours. Turn it off for a moment if you want the clock to find you.

Sección 3

Una compra, sin actualizaciones

La versión que compra es la versión que conserva.

RelojSolarLunar se compra una vez y es suya para siempre. No es una suscripción. No es un servicio. Es un producto terminado, completo el día que lo adquirió.

Por eso, no hay actualizaciones automáticas. La copia que tiene hoy en su máquina es la copia que tendrá mañana — funcionando en silencio, haciendo lo que siempre ha hecho.

Si algún día lanzamos una nueva edición — un motivo renovado, una temática distinta, un producto completamente nuevo — será una compra nueva. A los clientes de ediciones anteriores no se les migra, no se les empuja, no se les interrumpe. Lo que compró es lo que tiene.
